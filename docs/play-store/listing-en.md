## Full description (max 4000 characters)

**Your time, on purpose.**

Meotime helps you spend less time in the apps that quietly eat your day — and more time on everything else.

Pick the apps you want to keep an eye on, set a daily limit for each one, and let Meotime do the rest. When you reach a limit, a calm reminder appears over the app. Not a lecture, just a nudge that it might be time to stop. You decide whether to close the app or keep going.

No account. No cloud. No streak guilt.

**WHAT YOU CAN DO**

• Choose any installed app and set a daily limit in minutes
• See today's usage at a glance for every app you monitor
• Get an on-screen reminder the moment you hit a limit, even while the app is open
• Get a notification when Meotime isn't in the foreground
• Watch how your usage changes over time: totals, a comparison against the previous period, an hourly view for a single day, and a breakdown by app
• Look back at one day or any custom date range
• Change a limit, pause monitoring for an app, or remove it at any time
• Light and dark themes that follow your system setting

**HOW IT WORKS**

1. Grant usage access, display-over-other-apps, and notification permissions. Meotime explains each one before asking.
2. Add the apps you want to watch and set a daily limit.
3. Keep using your phone. Meotime counts time while a monitored app is in the foreground and steps in when you cross a limit.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**

Everything stays on your device. Meotime has no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no advertising SDK. Your usage history is stored locally and never leaves your phone.

**ABOUT PERMISSIONS**

Meotime uses usage access to measure how long monitored apps stay in the foreground, display-over-other-apps to show the limit reminder, and notifications to remind you when the app isn't open. It also reads your installed app list so you can choose what to monitor. Nothing is uploaded or shared.

**GOOD TO KNOW**

• Only the apps you choose are tracked. Meotime does not measure whole-device screen time.
• Usage is saved when a monitored session ends, so a crash or force-stop can lose the last few minutes of a session.
• Meotime is a self-control tool, not parental control software.
